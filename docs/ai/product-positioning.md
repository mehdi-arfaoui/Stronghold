# Product Positioning

Version: 1

Stronghold turns AWS disaster recovery posture into deterministic, inspectable recoverability evidence.

The product position is recoverability-as-code:

- Scan cloud infrastructure read-only.
- Model services as business capabilities.
- Validate deterministic DR rules and contracts.
- Require measured tested evidence for recovery objectives.
- Produce auditable reports, plans, and proof loops.

Stronghold should not be positioned as:

- An LLM decision engine.
- A telemetry product.
- An infrastructure mutator.
- A scanner that treats resources as the primary product unit.
- A tool that treats estimates as proof.
